# Welcome to my blog

I was raised on it by my parents and for some odd reason I feel that in comparison to my fellow youths, the great separation never really took place for me. This of course being the young person's rebellious act of distancing themselves from everything their predesesors created. Now this act may seem like a necessary step for many, after all to carve out your own identity you first have to distance yourself from what has been defining you in the past. 

Well I don't want to be one of those children that cry about being born in the wrong generation and how everything was so much better in the past.
